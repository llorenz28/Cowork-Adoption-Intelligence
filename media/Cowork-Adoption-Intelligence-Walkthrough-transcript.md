# Cowork Adoption Intelligence walkthrough transcript

The template is in testing, contains no embedded data, and is designed to load approved customer exports.

## Cowork Adoption Intelligence

Meet Cowork Adoption Intelligence: a testing, data-free Power BI template that turns approved Cowork exports into a clear adoption story. It connects reach, return, organizational patterns, workflow maturity, potential champions, work mix, and modeled assisted time.

## Start Here

Start Here routes program owners to the right question. Is adoption building momentum? Where is it strongest? How mature is usage? Begin with the decision, then open the page that carries the right evidence and interpretation boundary.

## Executive Summary

Executive Summary reads the latest active week. Weekly active users show reach. Prompts, return rate, and active days show habit. The momentum score balances return, consistency, intensity, and advanced delegation, so share it with the week, filters, weights, and caps.

## Weekly Adoption & Usage

Weekly Adoption and Usage shows the latest twelve active weeks. Switch among reach, momentum, prompts, active days, and return rate without clearing filters. Use the heatmap to find concentration, then check rollout timing, holidays, and export coverage before calling a trend.

## Adoption by Attributes

Adoption by Attributes compares observed task volume and delegation patterns by department or country. These views depend on optional organization enrichment. Always check population size and mapping coverage before comparing groups.

## User Maturity

User Maturity combines the delegation ladder, multi-skill task share, task duration, and rolling twelve-week usage tiers. Scheduled-task totals are lifetime-scoped, while the other rungs follow the selected period. Pair every maturity signal with role and work context.

## Cowork Champions

Cowork Champions identifies potential enablement partners, not top performers. Eligibility requires three observed threads across two active weeks. The evidence score balances activity, consistency, and delegation maturity, then applies the selected percentile tier.

## Activity & Assisted Hours

Activity and Assisted Hours connects observed Cowork records to low, mid, or high time-saved assumptions. Category results are allocated estimates, not per-skill metering or a time study. Present a range and label every result as modeled assisted time.

## What They Use It For

What They Use It For groups observed skills into work patterns and preserves action and user detail behind bookmarks. Action value tiers are declared category references, not user scores. Review unmapped skills and validate dominant categories before recommending a workflow.

## Momentum Score Guide

Momentum Score Guide explains the live adoption signal before exposing optional controls. The default weights are thirty-five percent return, twenty-five percent consistency, twenty-five percent intensity, and fifteen percent maturity. If the weights do not total one hundred percent, the score becomes blank.

## Adoption Metric Guide

The Adoption Metric Guide defines all ten report pages, with evidence status, thresholds, dependencies, and interpretation boundaries. Copy the definition, reporting period, filters, and status into decision records. This is decision support, not billing, compliance, or personnel evidence.

## One report. A clearer adoption story.

That is Cowork Adoption Intelligence: one testing template for reach, return, organizational patterns, workflow maturity, champions, work mix, and transparent assisted-time scenarios. Start with the fabricated sample, review the definitions, then connect approved exports when ready.
