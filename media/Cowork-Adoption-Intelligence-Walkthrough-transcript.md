# Cowork Adoption Intelligence walkthrough transcript

The template is in testing, contains no embedded data, and is designed to load approved customer exports.

## Cowork Adoption Intelligence

Meet Cowork Adoption Intelligence: a testing, data-free Power BI template that turns approved Cowork audit exports into a clear adoption story. See reach, return, delegated work, workflow maturity, potential champions, and modeled value. Here is a quick tour of the decisions it supports.

## Start Here

Start Here routes program owners to the right question: Is adoption growing? Are people returning? How deeply do they delegate? Where are potential champions? Begin with the decision, then follow the report to the right page and evidence.

## Executive Summary

Executive Summary reads the latest active week. Users and task threads show reach. Prompts, active days, and return rate show habit. The momentum score balances return, consistency, intensity, and advanced delegation, so always read it with its components.

## Weekly Adoption & Usage

Weekly Adoption and Usage shows the latest twelve active weeks. Switch metrics without clearing filters, and use the heatmap to find concentration. Compare the same period and population, and check rollout timing or export coverage before calling a trend.

## User Maturity

User Maturity combines rolling usage tiers, multi-skill task share, task duration, and the delegation ladder. These signals can show readiness for advanced workflows, but may also reflect retries or friction. Pair them with role and work context.

## Find potential champions responsibly

Cowork Champions identifies potential enablement partners, not top performers. Eligibility requires three threads across two active weeks. The score combines forty percent activity, thirty-five percent consistency, and twenty-five percent delegation maturity. Confirm role fit, willingness, and manager support before outreach.

## What They Use It For

What They Use It For groups observed skills into work patterns and exposes unmapped tools for taxonomy maintenance. Action value tiers are category references, not user scores. Validate the dominant categories before recommending a new workflow or enablement scenario.

## Activity & Assisted Hours

Activity and Assisted Hours applies selected low, mid, or high time benchmarks to observed task volume. Present a range, keep the assumptions visible, and label every result as modeled assisted time, not realized savings.

## Adoption Metric Guide

The Adoption Metric Guide keeps definitions, thresholds, dependencies, and boundaries visible. Use Usage Explorer to trace a headline to approved source records without assuming intent. Missing optional data means unavailable, not zero activity. This is decision support, not billing, compliance, or personnel evidence.

## One report. A clearer adoption story.

That is Cowork Adoption Intelligence: one testing template for reach, return, workflow maturity, champions, and transparent modeled value. Start with the fabricated sample, review the definitions, then connect approved exports to build an evidence-aware enablement plan.
